Setup steps and orientation for the workshop: the data platforms used during the workshop (USGS EarthExplorer, Copernicus Data Space, the Census API and GitHub), a short guide to QGIS, and a Python notebook refresher.

This module was taught as part of the Purdue-NSF GIS Cybertraining for the Social Sciences Workshop, a two-day workshop held at Purdue University in 2026 (Day 1 opening module, for all participants). See the [Workshop 2026 page](https://spatialturn.github.io/workshop2026.html) for the full program.

It is part of [Enabling the Geospatial Turn in the Social Sciences](https://spatialturn.github.io/), a set of training modules developed at Purdue University with support from the National Science Foundation.
